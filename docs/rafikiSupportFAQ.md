# RafikiSupport — Customer Support FAQ

**Document type:** Public Customer Support Knowledge Base
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Sample document — review before publishing

## 1. General Questions

### Q1. What is RafikiSupport?

RafikiSupport is a customer-support assistant designed to help customers find answers to common questions, understand available services, and get assistance with support issues.

### Q2. How can I get help from RafikiSupport?

Type your question into the chat window using clear, simple language. Include relevant details about your issue, but do not share passwords, PINs, or other sensitive information.

### Q3. What questions can RafikiSupport answer?

RafikiSupport can answer questions covered by its approved knowledge base, such as service information, frequently asked questions, troubleshooting guidance, and support procedures.

### Q4. What if RafikiSupport does not know the answer?

If the assistant cannot confidently answer a question, it may escalate the issue to a human support representative. Provide a brief description of the problem so the support team can understand your request.

### Q5. Is RafikiSupport available in other languages?

Language availability depends on the current service configuration. Ask your question in your preferred language to check whether the assistant can help.

## 2. Account and Access

### Q6. How do I create an account?

Follow the registration instructions on the relevant service website, if account registration is available. If you cannot find the registration option, contact the support team for guidance.

### Q7. I cannot sign in. What should I do?

Check that you entered the correct email address or username and password. If available, use the password-reset option. If you still cannot sign in, request assistance from the support team.

### Q8. How do I reset my password?

Use the official password-reset process provided by the service. Follow the instructions sent to your registered email address, if applicable. Never share your password or password-reset code in a support conversation.

### Q9. How do I update my account details?

Check your account settings for available profile-editing options. If the information cannot be changed directly, contact the support team for assistance.

### Q10. My account appears locked. What should I do?

Stop repeated sign-in attempts and follow the official account-recovery instructions. Contact support if the issue continues. Never send your password or authentication codes to a support agent.

## 3. Orders and Service Requests

### Q11. How can I place an order or submit a service request?

Use the official website or application and follow the instructions for the relevant service. If you encounter a problem, describe the service you need and the step where you became stuck.

### Q12. How can I check my order or request status?

Use the official order-tracking or request-status feature if one is available. If you need further assistance, provide your order or request reference through an approved support channel.

### Q13. My order or request is delayed. What should I do?

Check the latest status and any notifications you have received. If the delay remains unexplained, contact support with your reference number and a short description of the issue.

### Q14. I submitted a request but did not receive confirmation. What should I do?

Check your email, including the spam or junk folder, and review the request history if available. Avoid submitting duplicate requests until you have checked whether the original submission was successful.

### Q15. Can I cancel or change a request?

Cancellation and modification depend on the relevant service's policies and the current status of your request. Contact support with your reference number to confirm the available options.

## 4. Payments, Billing, and Refunds

### Q16. Which payment methods are accepted?

Accepted payment methods depend on the service. Check the official checkout page or payment instructions for the available options.

### Q17. My payment failed. What should I do?

Confirm that your payment details are correct and check the payment provider's notification. If money was deducted but the transaction failed, retain the transaction reference and contact the official support team. Avoid making repeated payments until the transaction status is clear.

### Q18. I was charged but my order was not confirmed. What should I do?

Keep your transaction reference, payment confirmation, and order details. Contact support to request verification of the payment and order status. Do not share your PIN, password, or one-time authentication code.

### Q19. How do I request a refund?

Follow the service's official refund procedure, if available. Refund eligibility, processing time, and required information depend on the applicable refund policy. Contact support with your transaction or order reference for guidance.

### Q20. How long does a refund take?

Refund timelines depend on the service's policy, the payment provider, and the transaction. Consult the official refund terms or contact support for the applicable timeframe.

### Q21. Where can I find my receipt or invoice?

Check your email confirmation, account order history, or the service's billing section, if available. If you cannot locate the document, ask support how to obtain a copy.

## 5. Technical Support and Troubleshooting

### Q22. The website is not loading. What should I do?

Check your internet connection, refresh the page, and try again. If the problem continues, try another browser or device. Contact support if the issue persists.

### Q23. A feature is not working. How can I report it?

Describe what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and any error message displayed. Include the page or feature involved and the approximate time of the problem.

### Q24. I keep receiving an error message. What information should I provide?

Provide the exact error message, the action that triggered it, and the steps you have already tried. A screenshot may help, provided it does not expose passwords, payment details, or other private information.

### Q25. The chatbot is not responding. What should I do?

Check your internet connection and wait briefly. If the chat remains unresponsive, refresh the page and try again. If the problem persists, use the service's official alternative support channel, if available.

### Q26. Will clearing my browser cache delete my account or orders?

Clearing cached website files generally removes temporary browser data, not server-stored account records or orders. However, clearing cookies or site data may sign you out or reset website preferences.

## 6. Privacy and Security

### Q27. What information should I avoid sharing in chat?

Never share passwords, PINs, one-time passwords, full payment-card details, secret authentication keys, or other credentials. Only provide personal or transaction information when it is necessary and requested through an approved, secure process.

### Q28. How is my personal information handled?

Personal information should be handled according to the service's published privacy policy and applicable data-protection requirements. Consult the official privacy policy for details about data collection, use, retention, and sharing.

### Q29. Can I request the deletion or correction of my personal information?

Use the service's official privacy or data-request procedure, if available. The support team can direct you to the appropriate process for submitting your request.

### Q30. How can I identify a suspicious support request?

Be cautious of anyone asking for your password, PIN, one-time code, or an unexpected payment. Verify support contacts using the service's official website or trusted communication channels.

## 7. Human Support and Escalations

### Q31. How do I contact a human support representative?

Ask the assistant to connect you with a human representative or request escalation. If a human-support option is available, follow the instructions provided.

### Q32. What information should I include when requesting support?

Include a concise description of the problem, the service or feature involved, relevant dates, and a reference number if applicable. Share sensitive information only through approved secure channels.

### Q33. How long does it take to receive a response?

Response times depend on the support team's operating hours, workload, and the type of issue. Refer to the service's official support information for current response-time expectations.

### Q34. What happens after my issue is escalated?

A support representative may review the information provided and contact you through the configured support channel. Keep your reference number, if one is provided, so you can follow up.

### Q35. Can I follow up on an unresolved issue?

Yes. Use the existing support ticket or request reference, if available, and explain what remains unresolved. Include any relevant updates since your previous contact.

## 8. Service Policies and Availability

### Q36. What are your operating hours?

Operating hours have not yet been specified in this sample knowledge base. Check the official service website or contact support for the current schedule.

### Q37. Where can I find your terms and conditions?

Consult the terms and conditions published on the official service website. These terms explain the applicable rules for using the service.

### Q38. Where can I find the latest service updates?

Check official announcements, service notifications, or the website's updates section, if available.

### Q39. Does RafikiSupport guarantee that every question will be answered?

No. The assistant can answer questions supported by its available knowledge and capabilities. Some requests require additional information or review by a human representative.

### Q40. What should I do if the answer does not solve my problem?

Explain what remains unresolved and provide any relevant context. Ask for escalation if necessary so a support representative can review the issue.

## Instructions for the Support Assistant

When answering questions using this document:

1. Give clear, concise answers that directly address the customer's question.
2. Use approved knowledge-base information as the source of truth.
3. Do not invent prices, policies, contact details, refund timelines, operating hours, or guarantees.
4. If a question depends on a customer's private order, payment, or account information, use an authorized lookup tool if available. Never expose another customer's information.
5. If the available information is insufficient, explain what is unknown and offer escalation where supported.
6. Never request passwords, PINs, or one-time authentication codes.
7. Do not claim that an order, payment, refund, or support ticket has been changed or created unless an authorized tool confirms the action.
8. When appropriate, cite the knowledge source used to answer the question.
